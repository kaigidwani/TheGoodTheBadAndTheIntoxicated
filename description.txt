The Good, The Bad, and The Intoxicated adds a new western themed dungeon quest including a dungeon, 2 new NPCs, 4 enemies, 1 boss, and 6 weapons all in their own new dimension! An old gunslinger gives you a map to the Tombstone Taphouse, where the bartender resides and asks you to kill 8 Ball Paul and the Billiard Boys in his cellar.

Contents:

Old Gunslinger NPC:
 - ﻿Moves in when you get ranged weapons or ammo
 - Sells map to Tombstone Taphouse to start mod
 - Sells weapons once 8 Ball Paul is defeated


Barkeeper NPC:
 - Lives in the Tombstone Taphouse
 - Sells the key to get into the Tombstone Taphouse basement
 - Moves in once 8 Ball Paul is defeated


Weapons:
 - Broken Bottle stabbing sword
 - The Rattler ricochet shotgun
 - Bounty Magic spell that increases damage on the next attack
 - Bolt Action Rifle
 - Lever Action Shotgun
 - Six Shooter Revolver


Items:
 - Bar Map to Tombstone Taphouse
 - Saloon Key to Taphouse basement
 - 8 Ball boss summon consumable


Enemies:
 - Number 3 - melee, drops Broken Bottle
 - Number 14 - ranged, drops Bolt Action Rifle
 - Number 2 - ranged, drops Lever Action Shotgun
 - Number 7 - ranged, drops Six Shooter Revolver


Boss - 8 Ball Paul:
 - Summoned using an 8 Ball in the Pool Table
 - Shoots you with all the other guns, and the Rattler!
 - Drops the Rattler when defeated.
 - After being defeated, the Old Gunslinger will start selling the weapons from the mod. Additionally, the Barkeeper can move in to your town.


This mod was created as a team project for a game modding class within The School of Interactive Games and Media (IGM) at Rochester Institute of Technology in Rochester NY. IGM provides an educational environment that supports and encourages creative and collaborative academic inquiry by both faculty and students. IGM’s programs,  coursework, research, and development efforts provide students with the knowledge and skills to pursue meaningful  and rewarding careers in media-centric, interactive software development, while simultaneously advancing the field and helping to provide a well-rounded educational experience.
